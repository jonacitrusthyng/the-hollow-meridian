# Hollow Meridian: signal — Illustrated Saga Edition v2.0.0

By Jona Citrus Thyng

*The Hollow Meridian* becomes **Hollow Meridian: signal**, the complete, free illustrated first novel in a saga planned for at least three books.

## The reading order

1. **Hollow Meridian: signal — The Unaltered Timeline.** Book One preserves the history lived before temporal intervention. The complete novel is free.
2. **Hollow Meridian: drift — The First Temporal Intervention.** Developed under the working title *Signal from 2176*. A four-point-seven-second signal connects 2176 and 2026. This release includes a preview.
3. **Hollow Meridian: fracture — The Price of Temporal Meddling.** The consequences of intervention and repeated time loops. Forthcoming.
