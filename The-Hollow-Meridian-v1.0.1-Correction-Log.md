# The Hollow Meridian — Corrected Edition v1.0.1

Author: Jona Citrus Thyng  
Revision date: August 24, 2026  
Status: Corrected free digital edition

## Editorial boundary

This edition preserves the title, all characters, plot, relationships, themes, narrative voice, nonlinear imagery, and ending. It is a bounded continuity and conversion repair, not a rewrite.

## Corrections made

- Standardized Ren's pronouns to **they/them/their** wherever Ren is the referent. Feminine pronouns referring to Mira in the same passages were preserved.
- Corrected Cassidy Reach's isolated masculine-pronoun drift.
- Restored manuscript text lost during the HTML-to-print conversion:
  - Mira's first private letter to Patience in Chapter 5.
  - Patience's first visible reply: *I remember the light.*
  - The lost Chapter 4 epigraph.
  - Six lost epigraph attributions and the Chapter 18 countdown line.
- Preserved both characters named Sera and made their identities explicit after both enter the story:
  - **Sera Novak**, the cartographer.
  - **Sera of the Chorus**, one of the Chorus's three members.
- Consolidated alternate-draft identity remnants without changing the characters:
  - Ossel → Sola.
  - Fenne → Fen for the 81-year-old cartographer; epigraph author Orla Fenne remains unchanged.
  - Ket → Pell.
- Reconciled Sola's signal-speech origin and Pell's mesh history.
- Reconciled objective age and chronology conflicts:
  - Theo is 41, consistent with integration at 23 and eighteen years in the mesh.
  - Covenant is 73 and first met the Architect 47 years earlier at age 26.
- Corrected the ceremony roster to **19 people**: 12 outside witnesses + 4 core participants + 3 Chorus members. No named or depicted participant was removed.
- Preserved the twelve lanterns and Chapter 26 title, *Twelve*, as deliberate symbols of the original outside-witness group.
- Reconciled isolated alternate-draft time and location remnants with the repeated 30-day/August/Gulf of Mexico chronology.
- Clarified that the gathered 7-EMBER cluster dissolved while remote Shard fragments remained.
- Repaired the malformed Chapter 4 title and normalized irregular epigraph formatting in the editable manuscript.
- Updated public-edition metadata and sharing language for v1.0.1.

## Canonical choices used

- Pell has used the mesh for eight months, matching the specific statement that she came to integration late.
- Theo is 41, preserving both repeated references to eighteen years in the mesh.
- The ceremony contains 19 people, preserving every explicitly depicted participant.

## Deliberately preserved

- Subjective, nonlinear, and time-bending imagery remains unchanged.
- Chapter 18's restored “Four days remaining” marker and Chapter 19's thematic title remain as authored; the title does not state that the ceremony occurs the following day.
- Perennial can be Mira's long-term duty residence while another flotilla remains home; both meanings of “home” remain intact.
- The original v1.0.0 release and the twenty first-print copies remain historical artifacts of that edition.
